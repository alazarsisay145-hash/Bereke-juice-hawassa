About Bereket

Made for Hawassa mornings, family catch-ups and late-day cravings.

Freshness, comfort and unmistakable Bereket energy.

Bereket Juice & Fruit Salad grew from a simple promise: serve fruit at peak freshness, blend with care, and make every stop feel genuinely welcoming.

From layered Spris juice to fruit salads and burgers, the shop brings together marble finishes, greenery, warm light and neighborly service in a space that feels easy to return to.

Fresh daily prep

Mango, avocado, papaya, banana, strawberry and more are prepared daily for juices, smoothies and salads.

Made your way

Quick pickup, easy sharing and satisfying burgers round out the fruit-forward menu for all-day orders.

Warm place to gather

Bright seating, marble accents and a family-friendly atmosphere make Bereket easy for both quick stops and longer catch-ups.
