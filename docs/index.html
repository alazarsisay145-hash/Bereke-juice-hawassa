About Bereket

Built around freshness, hospitality and Hawassa energy.

Bereket Juice & Salad grew from a simple promise: serve fruit at peak freshness, blend with care, and make every stop feel welcoming. From layered Spris juice to fruit salads and burgers, the shop has become a colorful part of Hawassa’s street life.

The real shop is bright, green and lively — from the fruit display outside the new storefront to the lantern glow, greenery and evening atmosphere of the original location.

Fresh daily prep

Mango, avocado, papaya, banana, strawberry and more are prepared daily for juices, smoothies and salads.

Street-to-lounge feel

A modern glass-front energy outside, with warm seating, marble tones and greenery-inspired hospitality inside.

Made for sharing

Perfect for family stops, quick refreshment, evening meetups and late-day burger cravings in Hawassa.
